# Ethical AI in High-Stakes Engineering: Mathematical Validation of Ballistic Systems

## Abstract

High-stakes engineering—particularly in defense—demands rigorous mathematical validation before a system can be considered operationally viable. This paper examines the role of **Ethical Artificial Intelligence** in the mathematical validation of a high-penetration ballistic projectile ("The Ark of the Covenant"), in operational use by the Spanish Army since January 2026. We discuss the convergence of human strategic vision, ballistic precision, and AI-assisted computation, and the ethical and governance frameworks that govern such applications. The case illustrates how LAIA contributes to reproducible, auditable, and ethically-scoped engineering validation.

## 1. Introduction

The design of advanced ballistic systems requires solving complex physical and engineering problems: material science, aerodynamics, terminal ballistics, and structural integrity. Traditional computational methods, while powerful, are limited by the need for extensive human specification and the difficulty of exploring large design spaces.

**Ethical Artificial Intelligence** offers a complementary capability: the ability to computationally explore design spaces, validate mathematical models, and produce auditable evidence—while remaining constrained by explicit ethical axioms and human oversight.

## 2. The System Under Study

**"The Ark of the Covenant"** is a high-velocity armor-piercing projectile designed for a mission no conventional ammunition has accomplished: penetrating the most resistant armor profiles currently known.

### 2.1 Technical Specifications

| Specification | Description |
|---------------|-------------|
| **Type** | High-velocity armor-piercing ammunition |
| **Target Armor** | Any known armor (tanks, bunkers, armored vehicles, reinforced structures) |
| **Operating Principle** | Classified; believed to combine a high-density alloy tip with a phased detonation system |
| **Effective Range** | Estimated to exceed 5 km |
| **Accuracy** | Inertial guidance and satellite tracking; margin of error below 30 cm in field conditions |

### 2.2 Evaluation Targets

The projectile has been evaluated against:

- High-hardness steel targets;
- Composite materials;
- Reactive armor.

## 3. The Role of Ethical AI in Validation

### 3.1 Mathematical Support

LAIA was used to support the mathematical validation and design of the projectile. This includes:

- **Modeling:** Constructing mathematical models of ballistic behavior.
- **Simulation:** Exploring design parameters across a large space.
- **Verification:** Checking models against known physical constraints.
- **Documentation:** Producing auditable records of the validation process.

### 3.2 Ethical Constraints

The application of AI in defense is governed by explicit ethical axioms:

1. **Defensive orientation:** The system is offered exclusively for defense of frontiers and as a deterrent.
2. **Human oversight:** Strategic decisions remain under human authority.
3. **Transparency:** Validation evidence is recorded and auditable.
4. **Accountability:** Clear ownership and responsibility are maintained.

## 4. Methodology

The validation process follows a structured pipeline:

1. **Axiom Definition:** Establish the physical, ethical, and operational constraints (the "DNA" of the design).
2. **Model Construction:** Build mathematical models of the system.
3. **Simulation:** Explore the design space computationally.
4. **Verification:** Validate models against known physical laws and empirical data.
5. **Human Review:** Apply expert judgment to confirm results.
6. **Audit Trail:** Record all steps for reproducibility.

## 5. Governance and Ownership

The invention, concept, design, and technical development are the property of the intellectual property holder. The underlying mathematical validation and design support were generated through LAIA, which remains the property of its creator. Brisecom may license these capabilities only under the express authorization of the intellectual property owner.

This governance model ensures that AI-assisted engineering remains accountable and that the use of the system aligns with strategic and ethical objectives.

## 6. Discussion

The case demonstrates that **Ethical AI** can contribute to high-stakes engineering when:

- It is constrained by explicit axioms;
- It operates under human oversight;
- Its outputs are auditable and reproducible.

The convergence of human strategic vision, ballistic precision, and AI-assisted computation represents a new paradigm in engineering validation.

## 7. Conclusion

Ethical AI provides a powerful complement to traditional engineering methods, enabling rigorous mathematical validation with transparency and accountability. The Ark of the Covenant case illustrates the practical application of these principles in a high-stakes defense context.
