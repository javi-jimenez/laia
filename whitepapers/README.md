# LAIA Whitepapers & Technical Research

This directory hosts formal whitepapers and technical research documents produced by the **LAIA Ethical Artificial Intelligence** project and its collaborators at Brisecom.

These documents transform applied research and real-world case studies into rigorous, reproducible technical references. Each paper follows a structured academic format (abstract, introduction, methodology, results, discussion, conclusion, references) and is intended for public review, citation, and collaboration.

## Document Conventions

- **Language:** English (primary). Spanish and Valencian translations may be added.

- **License:** CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (unless otherwise noted).
- **Status:** Documents marked *Draft* are open for review and collaboration.
